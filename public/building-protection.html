Area 01 / 06Building protection

Terrace & roof

The most exposed surface on the building: sun, heat, monsoon ponding and constant thermal movement.

What you might be noticing

Damp patches or stains on the top-floor ceiling, worse after heavy rain, and water that stands on the terrace for hours.

What usually causes it
  • Ponding from poor slope
  • UV and thermal cycling
  • Cracked screed
  • Failed parapet and outlet details
What we would consider
  • Liquid-applied membranes
  • Reinforced detailing at upturns
  • UV-stable topcoats
What has to be right on site
  • Slope and drainage correction
  • Coving at parapets
  • Outlet and penetration detailing
