Pillar 03Permeable surfaces

Surfaces that work with water.Not against it.

Most external surfaces send rain straight to a drain. A properly designed permeable surface lets it pass through, holds it below and, where the ground allows, lets it soak back in.

ConventionalRain runs off.

A sealed surface sends every drop across the top and into a drain. Nothing is stored and nothing reaches the ground.

PermeableRain passes through.

Water drains through the surface, is held in an aggregate sub-base and, where the ground allows, soaks back in.

Whether a site suits a permeable surface depends on the soil, groundwater, slope, traffic and maintenance. We assess it first, and say so when it is not suitable.
