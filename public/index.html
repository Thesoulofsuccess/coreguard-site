How we handle your enquiry.Plain language, before you send.

What happens to the details you share with us, and how to change your mind. Draft wording, to be reviewed against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules before launch.

01

What we collect

Your name, phone number, and anything you choose to add: email, locality or company, the type of property and a description of the problem.

02

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry, arrange an assessment and send a proposal. We do not sell it or use it for unrelated marketing.

03

Where it goes

The form opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you press send. WhatsApp's own terms apply to that message.

04

How long we keep it

If your enquiry does not become a project, your details are deleted within 12 months. If it does, project records are kept only as long as tax and legal requirements oblige us to.

05

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, at any time. Withdrawing does not affect work already agreed.

06

Who to contact

To see the details we hold about you, correct or delete them, withdraw your consent, or raise a concern about how they were used, write to Pankaj Deshmukh or Vikash Rajan at info@coreguard.in. We will reply within 7 working days.
